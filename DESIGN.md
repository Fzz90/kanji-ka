# Kanji 可 (Tema Bulan & Matahari)

## Brief
Five interactive presentation slides in Indonesian for **Faiz Syihab, NIM 125241046, Kelas A** (`Kanji Jawir`). Presents Kanji **可** following every slide structure of `122_Raina sirvi falihah.pdf` (Cover, Meanings/Readings/Radicals + Stroke Order, Mnemonics `覚え方`, Compound Words & Sentences including **可愛い**, and Closing `ありがとう`), featuring an animated 5-stroke **KanjiVG** brush-writing player, toggleable ruby furigana, and a **Celestial Sun & Moon (*Nichigetsu / 日月*)** visual direction inspired by [bunpou-nodeshita](https://github.com/Fzz90/bunpou-nodeshita).

## Design Read & Dials
Reading this as: **interactive kanji study & stroke-animation presentation deck** for **university learners**, in a **Sun & Moon Japanese editorial almanac (*Nichigetsu*) visual language** following `bunpou-nodeshita`'s crisp ink-framed panel discipline, dial **ENERGY 3 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 2**.

## Direction Note (`cx-design-taste-frontend`)
Drawn direction: **`page-is-the-object`** (the presentation stage functions as an interactive Japanese celestial kanji sheet & stroke manuscript). Type (`Bricolage Grotesque` + `Zen Kaku Gothic New`) and flat palette were redrawn from the default draw sheet to honor the required `bunpou-nodeshita` theme lineage and the Moon & Sun brief while strictly avoiding gradients, blurred glass blobs, cream+terracotta, and violet/indigo hues.

- **Midnight Ink (`--ink`)**: `#121622` (crisp `3px` frames & primary dark text; `16.55:1` on paper, `18.05:1` on white)

- **Solar Hinomaru Crimson (`--sun`)**: `#C41E2A` (Matahari primary accent, stroke highlight, primary CTA; `5.89:1` with white, `5.40:1` on paper)
- **Solar Gold / Moonbeam (`--gold`)**: `#FAE46B` (Sun halo & crescent moon accent, paired with `#524100` bronze ink `7.74:1`, `#121622` ink, or `#0C254D` `11.80:1`)
- **Lunar Prussian Blue (`--moon` / `--moon-deep`)**: `#123A7A` / `#0C254D` (Bulan night sky fields & contrasting panels; `15.14:1` white on `--moon-deep`)
- **Type**: `Bricolage Grotesque` (`500;700;800` for Latin headings & Indonesian UI) + `Zen Kaku Gothic New` (`500;700;900` for Japanese kanji, readings, & furigana).

## Slide Map (Mirrors Reference PDF 1..5)
1. **Pembuka (`可`)**: Split cover pairing giant kanji **`可`**, presenter details (**Faiz Syihab · NIM 125241046 · Kelas A**), and custom dual-panel **Matahari & Bulan** sunrise/midnight SVG illustration with `Mulai materi`.
2. **Cara Menulis & Info (`書き順 · 意味`)**: Left Solar panel hosts the live animated 5-stroke **KanjiVG** brush writer (`Putar/Jeda`, `Ulang`, stroke-by-stroke pen nib path animation + 5 clickable progressive thumbnails `1..5`). Right Lunar midnight panel lists `意味`, `訓読み`, `音読み`, `部首`, and `Strokes: 5`.
3. **Cara Mengingat (`覚え方`)**: Interactive visual component formula **`一 + 口 + 亅` (atau `丁 + 口`) = `可`** plus two contrasting story panels explaining visual origin & phonetic family (`河`, `歌`, `何`, `荷`), with live component-highlighting buttons inside an inline kanji inspector.
4. **Kosakata & Contoh (`熟語と例文`)**: Six contextual jukugo & sentence frames (`可愛い`, `可能`, `許可`, `不可能`, `可能性`, `可決`) with toggleable `<ruby><rt>` furigana and Indonesian translations.
5. **Penutup (`ありがとう`)**: Full-bleed midnight Lunar sky end-card with radiant Sun & Crescent Moon eclipse emblem, gold **`ありがとう。`** headline, signature, KanjiVG CC BY-SA 3.0 credit, and `Kembali ke awal` button.
